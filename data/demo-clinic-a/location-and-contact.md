## Public Transit and Landmarks

- Nearest Metro Station: Green Park Metro Station (Yellow Line), Gate Number 2, approximately 250 meters walking distance.
- Bus Stop: Green Park Plaza Stop, serviced by route numbers 505, 516, and 615.
- Parking: Two-wheeler and four-wheeler visitor parking is available in the basement level of Green Park Medical Plaza.

## Contact Information and Inquiries

- Reception and Information Desk: Dedicated staff are available on-site during clinic operating hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
- Emergency Guidance: In case of acute chest pain, breathing difficulties, or severe trauma, do not travel to the outpatient clinic. Call 112 or visit the nearest tertiary hospital emergency room immediately.
- Online Inquiries: Patients may use our interactive AI assistant on the clinic website to check real-time doctor availability and book appointments.
