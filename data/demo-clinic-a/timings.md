## Sunday and Holiday Policy

The clinic remains strictly closed on Sundays and designated national holidays. No doctor consultations or diagnostic facilities operate on Sundays. If you need medical attention on a Sunday or outside operational hours, please visit your nearest hospital emergency department or dial 112 immediately.
