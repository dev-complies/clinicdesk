# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers regarding consultations, doctor timings, booking, and clinic facilities at Demo Clinic A.

## Appointments and Scheduling

Do I need an appointment before visiting the clinic?
Yes. We strongly recommend booking your 30-minute consultation slot in advance to avoid long waiting times. Walk-in visitors are accommodated only if a doctor has an unbooked slot or a cancellation.

Kya Sunday ko clinic khula rehta hai?
Nahi, Sunday ko clinic poori tarah band rehta hai. Demo Clinic A Monday se Saturday subah 9:00 baje se shaam 5:00 baje tak khula rehta hai.

Appointment cancel ya reschedule kaise karein?
Aap website par hamare chat assistant se baat karke ya reception par phone karke apna appointment reschedule ya cancel kar sakte hain. Reschedule karne ke liye wahi mobile number provide karein jo booking ke waqt use kiya gaya tha.

## Doctor Consultations and Specialities

Which doctors are available at Demo Clinic A?
Dr. Priya Sharma specializes in Cardiology with consultation fee Rs. 800. Dr. Rahul Mehta specializes in Orthopedics with consultation fee Rs. 600.

Kya doctor online ya phone par dawai prescribe karte hain?
Nahi. In-person physical examination zaroori hota hai. Doctor prescription sirf clinic aane ke baad hi digital format mein issue kiya jata hai.

Is emergency cardiac care available at the clinic?
No. Demo Clinic A is an outpatient polyclinic and does not operate an emergency resuscitation ward. For acute emergencies like suspected heart attacks or severe bleeding, call 112 immediately.

## Clinic Facilities and Parking

Is wheelchair accessibility available?
Yes. Green Park Medical Plaza has wheelchair ramps at the ground entrance and stretcher-compatible elevators to the second floor.

Are diagnostic reports shared on WhatsApp or email?
Digital copies of ECG and X-Ray reports are sent via SMS link and email within 1 hour of test completion. Physical printed films are also provided at the counter.
