# Clinic Policies and Patient Guidelines

To ensure smooth service and minimal wait times for all patients, Demo Clinic A maintains clear scheduling and operational policies.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

- Advance Notice: Patients may reschedule or cancel their appointment up to 2 hours prior to the scheduled slot without penalty.
- Identity Verification: For your security, cancelling or rescheduling an appointment requires verifying the 10-digit mobile phone number provided during booking.
- Multiple Bookings: Each patient may maintain a maximum of 3 active future bookings across the clinic to prevent slot hoarding.
- No-Show Guideline: Repeated missed appointments without prior notice may require advance payment for future bookings.

## Late Arrival Policy

- Grace Period: A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed past the scheduled appointment time.
- Delayed Entry: If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment slot may be released to waiting walk-in patients, or you may be accommodated at the next available opening during the doctor's shift.

## What to Bring for Your Consultation

- A valid government-issued photo ID (such as Aadhaar card, PAN card, or driver's license).
- Previous medical records, hospital discharge summaries, and ongoing medication lists.
- Recent diagnostic lab reports, ECG tracings, or X-ray films if available.

## Accepted Payment Methods

- UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
- Debit and credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay).
- Cash payments at the reception billing counter.
