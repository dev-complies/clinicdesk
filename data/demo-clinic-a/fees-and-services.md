# Consultation Fees and Clinical Services

Demo Clinic A provides transparent and standardized outpatient pricing for all consultations and primary diagnostic procedures.

## Specialist Consultation Fees

| Doctor | Speciality | Consultation Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dr. Priya Sharma | Cardiology | Rs. 800 |
| Dr. Rahul Mehta | Orthopedics | Rs. 600 |

Consultation fees are payable at the front desk upon arrival. Each consultation fee covers one complete outpatient review including review of previous diagnostic records and digital prescription issuance. Follow-up reviews within 7 calendar days are charged at a discounted rate of 50 percent of the standard fee.

## Diagnostic Services Available

- Resting 12-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG): Rs. 350
- Digital Orthopedic X-Ray (Single View): Rs. 400
- Digital Orthopedic X-Ray (Two Views): Rs. 700
- Blood Pressure and Blood Glucose Screening: Complimentary during scheduled doctor visits
- Basic Joint Mobilization and Plaster Removal: Rs. 500

All diagnostic tests are performed by certified technicians on modern digital equipment. Results for ECG and X-Ray are provided within 30 minutes to facilitate same-day doctor review.
